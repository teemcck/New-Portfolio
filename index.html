Overview

Hi there! I'm Tristan, an engineering student studying computer science at the University of Michigan. I have a passion for software engineering and video game development. I am currently the programming director for WolverineSoft, a ~20 member game development studio on campus. My goal is to create unique, memorable experiences through games that motivate others to explore their passions. I have many things yet to learn and use this site to document the advances I make in my journey.

Current Pursuits

My software interests shift quite a bit. Right now, I am quite interested in computer architecture and am researching how I may develop my own ISA, assembler, linker, and emulator. I worked on implementing a similar toolchain for EECS370, Intro to Computer Organization. However, the main difference is that I previously implemented an mock (albiet turing complete) instruction set, LC2K, which is a reduced instruction set designed by EECS370 staff to make learning the concepts of assembly easier.

While I have not yet figured the exact logistics of my own instruction set, I would like to support a wider array of instructions then those offered by LC2K. Another direction I am thinking of taking this project is emulating an existing ISA for a more complicated chip then the one designed for LC2K. Recently, I saw a project emulating the 6502 Instruction Set, which was ran on early technology such as he Commodore 64, Atari 2600, and the NES. While the 6502 may be a bit beyond my capabilities as someone familiarizing themselves with the computer architecture space, I do believe it would still be quite fun to find another chip to emulate.
